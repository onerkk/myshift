## Final prompts

### lagoon-day
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a production background asset for a fullscreen immersive weather web app. Vertical 2:3 photograph, high resolution. A serene Taiwanese coastal wetland at blue golden transition daylight, looking across a broad still lagoon. Horizon very low at 78% image height, vast airy sky above with softly layered believable cumulus clouds mainly towards the horizon, upper sky mostly open blue grey with subtle warm light from upper right, no visible sun disk. Bottom fifth is extremely realistic softly rippled water reflecting sky, a distant low shoreline and sparse silhouetted reeds along bottom corners. Atmospheric depth, natural optical softness, cinematic editorial landscape photography, nuanced silver blue, muted teal and pale warm ivory highlights, no oversaturation. No people, buildings, mountains, UI, typography, letters, frames or watermarks. This is an environmental background texture, not a UI mockup. Natural photographic detail, absolutely not illustration or flat vector.

Extremely realistic macro frost crystals growing inward ONLY from the left and right outer edges, each edge occupies at most 18% of image width, sparse frost at top corners. The middle 64% and entire lower middle are fully transparent, absolutely no opaque background. Fine irregular feathery fern-like ice dendrites, microscopic crystalline facets, patches of icy translucent haze close to edges, tiny condensate droplets, anisotropic silver white highlights and subtle cool cyan shadows. Need real alpha transparency between individual ice crystals and in the centre; keep highlights luminous but detailed.

Keep large transparent areas between cracks. Genuine alpha background.
