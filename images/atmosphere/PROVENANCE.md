# Observatory assets

Created using the built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-19. These are generated visual assets, not documentary photographs or an actual-location live feed. Originals remain outside the release; runtime copies were transcoded to WebP using ffmpeg. Alpha transparency was retained for the glass textures.

Runtime paths:
- images/atmosphere/lagoon-day.webp
- images/atmosphere/lagoon-storm.webp
- images/atmosphere/frost-glass.webp
- images/atmosphere/glass-impact.webp

## Final prompts

### lagoon-day
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a production background asset for a fullscreen immersive weather web app. Vertical 2:3 photograph, high resolution. A serene Taiwanese coastal wetland at blue golden transition daylight, looking across a broad still lagoon. Horizon very low at 78% image height, vast airy sky above with softly layered believable cumulus clouds mainly towards the horizon, upper sky mostly open blue grey with subtle warm light from upper right, no visible sun disk. Bottom fifth is extremely realistic softly rippled water reflecting sky, a distant low shoreline and sparse silhouetted reeds along bottom corners. Atmospheric depth, natural optical softness, cinematic editorial landscape photography, nuanced silver blue, muted teal and pale warm ivory highlights, no oversaturation. No people, buildings, mountains, UI, typography, letters, frames or watermarks. This is an environmental background texture, not a UI mockup. Natural photographic detail, absolutely not illustration or flat vector.

### lagoon-storm
Use case photorealistic-natural. Production background asset for immersive weather web application, portrait 2:3. Cinematic real landscape photograph of Taiwanese coastal wetland lagoon under an approaching severe storm. Very low flat shoreline horizon at 78% of image height. Vast towering dark layered storm cloud ceiling, sculpted slate gray turbulent cloud volume, low scud and distant rain curtains over water, subtle silver rim light behind clouds, blue charcoal storm palette. Wind roughened lagoon with realistic short choppy waves and sparse dark reeds at bottom corners. No sun, no lightning bolt, no visible rain streaks in foreground, no people, buildings, mountains, letters, UI, frames or watermark. Photorealistic natural optics with immense depth, NOT illustration, NOT fantasy, no tornado funnel. Dark but readable intricate midtone cloud textures. Designed to have animated rain added programmatically.

### frost-glass (transparent background)
Use case photorealistic-natural. Production VFX overlay texture for freezing glass, portrait 2:3 on genuinely transparent background. Extremely realistic macro frost crystals growing inward ONLY from the left and right outer edges, each edge occupies at most 18% of image width, sparse frost at top corners. The middle 64% and entire lower middle are fully transparent, absolutely no opaque background. Fine irregular feathery fern-like ice dendrites, microscopic crystalline facets, patches of icy translucent haze close to edges, tiny condensate droplets, anisotropic silver white highlights and subtle cool cyan shadows. Natural irregular branching, varied density, looks like a real winter window photograph, never a regular geometric snowflake diagram. No window frame, no scene, no text, no border line, no UI. Need real alpha transparency between individual ice crystals and in the centre; keep highlights luminous but detailed.

### glass-impact (transparent background)
Use case photorealistic-natural. Production VFX sprite: a single realistic small impact fracture in a clear glass pane, isolated on true transparent background. Square composition, impact slightly left of center, naturally irregular radial hairline cracks spreading outward across 75 percent of the square, only a few asymmetric polygonal connecting fractures, very fine branching at tips, tiny crushed crystalline impact core, sharp thin silver white edge reflections with restrained dark blue optical shadows. Glass remains mostly clear and transparent, no opaque pane, no blue backdrop, no black backdrop. Like real hailstone damage on a window, detailed optical refraction glints, fine subtle cracks with variable thickness, not a spider web drawing, not a bullet hole, no smoke, no flying large shards, no frame, no words, no UI. Keep large transparent areas between cracks. Genuine alpha background.
